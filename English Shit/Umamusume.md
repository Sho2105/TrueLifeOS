# Umamusume Cheatsheet: Crush the URA Finals

## Core Strategy
- **Friendship First**: Year 1, max support card bonds (check top-right for linked supports). Hit 80% (orange) for rainbow "friendship training" = big stat boosts.
- **Minimize Resting**: Pick energy-restoring event options (e.g., "rest" in recurring train/break event unless energy’s full). Saves turns.
- **Training Levels**: Each training starts at level 1, levels up every 4 sessions. Summer camps (July/Aug, Years 2-3) auto-boost to level 5—enter with full energy. Mix in WIT training to manage stamina drain.
- **New Year Event**: Jan Year 2 (2nd choice) and Year 3 (1st choice) restore energy. Enter with mid/low energy. Late Jan lottery can also restore energy/mood.
- **G1 Races**: Max G1 races for skill points + fans. Fans level up unique skills (60k by Feb Year 3, 70k by Apr Year 3, 120k by Dc Year 3). Higher fans = better end rating. Use gametora.com to filter G1 races by terrain/distance.
- **Plan Events**: Check gametora’s event viewer for your Umamusume’s events. E.g., Daiwa Scarlet’s mood-down event—use Tazuna SSR’s 5 date events to restore energy/mood.

## Legacy Umamusume
- **What**: Post-URA, pick 2 legacy Umamusume to inherit sparks (attributes/proficiency).
- **Attgfmhgribute Sparks**: 3-star spark = +21 to stat (e.g., SPD). Two 3-star SPD parents + grandparents = +126 SPD at start. Apr Years 2-3, ~30% chance to inherit more (~+200 SPD total).
- **Proficiency Sparks**: Boost track/distance/position rating (e.g., Oguri Cap’s Dirt B to A/S).
- **3-Star Spark Tips**:
  - Min 600 in attribute (1000+ for specific spark).
  - “All 600” strategy: Train all attributes to 600 for random 3-star sparks.
  - Luck matters.
- **Borrow**: Use friend’s 3-star spark Umamusume.

## Skill Selection
- **Stamina First**: Low STA? Prioritize restoration skills.
- **Gold Skills**: Way better than regular. Grab all relevant ones (e.g., Super Creek’s Swinging Maestro = long races with mid STA).
- **Keywords**: “Greatly” > “Moderately” > “Slightly”. Avoid “Slightly” skills costing 180 points (no hint) unless desperate.
- **Top Skill**: Symboli Rudolf’s unique skill (“Greatly increase velocity”) = global meta.

## Pro Tips
- **Friends**: Join Discord (official/unofficial) or subreddit for friend IDs with MLB support cards or 3-star spark Umamusume. Boosts runs instantly.
- **Support Cards**: Match card attribute to training (e.g., 4-5 SPD cards for Sakura Bakushin O to max rainbow SPD training).
- **Low Rarity Viable**: Nice Nature = PvP meta (debuffs). Haru Urara = Dirt track staple. Upgrade Normal/SR to 3 stars for unique skills.
- **Race Planning**: Global lacks race planner, so use gametora.com. Balance training (attributes) vs. racing (skill points).
- **Summer Prep**: Full energy for summer camp. Avoid 4 straight trainings—mix in WIT.
- **Mood Management**: Tazuna SSR’s dates fix mood/energy. Plan for mood-down events.
